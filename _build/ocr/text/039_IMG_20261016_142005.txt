General Guidance on Parallel Tuning
1.Model size guides the initial layout.
We can estimate the staticpartbudget and choose an initial layout.
Reserve headroom for activations andbuffers.
Prefer EP for experts,use PP across domains,and add TP if necessary.
2.Context length guides Cp
Context length affectstheactivation and buffersize.
3.Topology guides placement
Keep communication-heavy groups (such as EP,TP, and CP) within NVLink domains where practical for better throughput.
For our256-GPU DeepSeek-V4-Pro runs,weuse PP4 and EP64 as the initial layout.
